TTS System in Baseball Broadcast Scenario
Mar 2019 - Mar 2021 (@NCSOFT)

At NCSOFT, we studied a "broadcasting-style" TTS system that can imitate sportscasters. To express the diverse speaking style of baseball broadcasting, especially in pitch ranges, expressive speech synthesis technique was needed. We studied a TTS system that can generate speech with various emotions and its intensity. Furthermore, we also developed the TTS system to generate speech with proper prosody corresponding to text symbols (,  ~  !  ?).

The speech samples generated from a neutral-style TTS system and our broadcasting-style TTS system can be found below. The content of the speech samples is the same.
